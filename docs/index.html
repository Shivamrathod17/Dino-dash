Privacy Policy for Dino Dash

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Summary: Dino Dash is an offline casual arcade game. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, location data, or device identifiers.

1. Information Collection and Use

Dino Dash is designed to respect your privacy:

  • No Personal Data: We do not require registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, or names.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not access your GPS or coarse location.
  • Local Gameplay Data Only: Game scores, collected coins, and audio settings are saved strictly locally on your device storage using encrypted/integrity-verified keys. This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services

The app operates offline and does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral ad tracking SDKs.

3. Children's Privacy

Dino Dash does not address anyone under the age of 13 to collect personal information. Since we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, our game is safe for family and children gameplay.

4. Permissions

The application requests only basic permissions necessary for running casual gameplay on Android (vibration haptics, basic audio playback settings). No sensitive permissions (such as microphone, camera, contacts, or external storage) are requested or used.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
